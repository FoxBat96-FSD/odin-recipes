Wilhelms' Delicious Bolognese Recipe

Home

This recipe's origin is from an Italian cook book I found in the pantry as a child and dates back to the 1980's

I have fine tuned this recipe over many years, adding a few extra ingredients here and there to make it more tasty

It should be more than enough te feed 6 people, please feel free to make it your own

Ingrediets

  • 2 Large Chopped Onions
  • 3 Stalks of Celery Diced
  • 3 Large Carriots Sliced Julliene or Grated
  • 1 ½ Cups of Red Wine
  • 500g of Minced Beef
  • 500g of Minced Pork
  • 4 Cans of Chopped Italian Tomatoes
  • 1 litre/500ml of Italian Passata(depending how saucy you want it)
  • ½ a Table Spoon of brown/white sugar
  • 2 Heaped Table Spoons of Italian Herbs
  • 1 Table Spoon of Ground Black Pepper
  • 1 to 2 Table Spoons of Sea Salt( do a taste test to add more or less later on)
  • 5 Table Spoons Extra Virgin Olive Oil

Cooking Instructions

  1. Heat Olive Oil in Large Saucepanor or Pot
  2. Fry Onion Until Caramalised or Translucent
  3. Add Celery and Carrot and Fry Until Soft
  4. Add Minced Beef and Pork and Fry Until Brown
  5. Stir In Half of the Sea Salt
  6. Stir In Half of the Italian Herbs
  7. Stir in Half of the Black Pepper
  8. Add the Red Wine and Cook Until Almost Evaporated
  9. Add 4 Cans of Tomatoes and Italian Passata
  10. Stir in The Sugar
  11. Add Water in Two of The The Empty Cans and Passata Containers, Rinse Out Into Each Other and Pour That Water Into Pot or Saucepan
  12. Alternatively You Can Also Use Your Pasta Water to Rinse out Your Containers, It Helps Thicken Your Sauce
  13. Simmer on Medium Heat for 35 Minutes, and Stir Occasionally
  14. Add the Rest Of Your Herbs, Black Pepper and Salt (Taste And Add The Rest of The Salt If Required)
  15. Simmer on Low For Another 30 Minutes, Check The Consistency of Your Sauce and Add More Water or Pasta Water If Needed
